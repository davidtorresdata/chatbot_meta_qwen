# ACME Corp - Products FAQ

## Product catalog
ACME offers the Standard Widget and the Premium Widget Pro.
The Standard Widget is available in blue and red. The Premium Widget Pro is available
in black, white and silver and includes an extended two-year warranty.

## Warranty
All ACME widgets include a one-year warranty. The Premium Widget Pro adds an
extended warranty for a total of two years. The warranty covers manufacturing
defects but does not cover damage caused by misuse, water, or unauthorized repairs.

## Shipping and delivery
Orders placed before 14:00 are shipped the same business day. Standard shipping
takes 3 to 5 business days. Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days and costs
an additional $15. Shipping is free for orders over $100.

## Payments
We accept all major credit cards, PayPal and bank transfer. Invoices are available
in the customer portal within 24 hours of purchase. Corporate accounts can pay
with net-30 terms after credit approval.
